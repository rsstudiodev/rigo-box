## 2. Positioning

Rigo Box is a neighborhood boxing gym, not a boutique fitness studio. It serves three
levels in the same room: recreational, amateur, and professional. The bio opens the door
to every age, so the gym sells access and community, not exclusivity.

Core promise: anyone can start here, and a real competitor can also train here.

## 3. Audience segments

1. **Kids** — highlights `clase niños` and `niños`. Parents are the buyers.
2. **Adults, recreational** — fitness, weight loss, stress relief, no competition goal.
3. **Amateur and professional fighters** — highlights `profesionales`, `Aldo Leonel`,
   `Angel H. García`, `Andy Daniel Gar`. Named athletes act as social proof.
4. **Families** — highlight `Familia boxeo`. Several members of one family train together.

## 4. Content themes (from story highlights and grid)

Highlights: `2026`, `Angel H. García`, `clase niños`, `somos RigoBox`, `Aldo Leonel`,
`profesionales`, `si`, `Todos ustedes`, `HechosenRigoBox`, `Manopleo`, `niños`,
`Andy Daniel Gar`, `Familia boxeo`, `remodelando`.

Read from these:

- **Athlete spotlights.** Individual fighters get their own highlight. The gym builds people.
- **`HechosenRigoBox`.** A "made here" claim. Strong tagline candidate for the website.
- **`somos RigoBox` / `Todos ustedes`.** Community identity, member gratitude.
- **`Manopleo`.** Pad work, the technical core of the training.
- **`remodelando`.** The facility is growing. Worth a "nuestras instalaciones" section.
- **Grid.** Promo flyers, group photos in the gym, sparring in the ring, real competition
  bouts under arena lights.

## 5. Visual identity

### Logo
Black circle, red boxing gloves hanging from a white hook, white wordmark `RIGO BOX`
in two lines, split by the gloves. Green four-point star accents. Thin white ring border.

### Color palette (observed)

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Near-black `#0B0B0B` | Backgrounds, logo field |
| Primary | Glove red `#C81E1E` | Accents, calls to action, headlines |
| Contrast | White `#FFFFFF` | Text, outlines |
| Accent | Star green `#3CB44A` | Sparse detail, badges |
| Neutral | Warm gray | Photo backgrounds, gym floor |

### Typography direction
Condensed, heavy, uppercase display type with outline or shadow. Italic slant for urgency.
Boxing-poster lineage. Body copy stays neutral and readable, so the impact stays with
headlines only.

### Photography
Real, unretouched gym interiors. Warm artificial light, exposed structure, red ring ropes,
crowded ringside shots. No stock imagery. Keep grain and motion blur; they read as authentic.

## 6. Tone of voice

Direct, warm, motivational, informal Spanish, `tú` form. Exclamation marks are on-brand.
Observed flyer copy, verbatim:

```
¡ENTRENA! ¡SUPÉRATE! Y SÉ LA MEJOR VERSIÓN DE TI
TODO LO QUE RESTA DE JUNIO SOLO PARA PERSONAS DE NUEVO INGRESO
```

Rules for new copy:
- Speak to the reader as `tú`.
- Short imperative verbs: `Entrena`, `Ven`, `Aprende`, `Agenda tu clase`.
- No corporate or fitness-influencer vocabulary.
- Keep the inclusive claim: `para todas las edades`.

## 7. Gaps found

- No website link in the bio. The site fills a real gap.
- No published price list.
- No class schedule beyond `L-V 6am-9pm`.
- `OpenBox` is not explained; the bio is truncated. Confirm the meaning with the owner.
- No WhatsApp or phone number visible on the profile.
